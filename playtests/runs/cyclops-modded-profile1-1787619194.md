## Checkpoints

### E20 - Act 1 Floor 7 Elite

- HP: 10/70; gold: 169 before rewards.
- Deck: 4x Strike, 4x Defend, Optic Breach, Field Training, We've Trained for This, Rapid Punch, Plan Everything, Break Their Guard, Danger Room.
- Upgrades: none.
- Relics: Cyclops Battle Visor, Booming Conch; Byrdonis relic reward pending.
- Potions: none before rewards.
- Central engine: Danger Room supplies temporary Strength and Dexterity each turn; Rapid Punch and Optic Breach amplify the offensive half, while Field Training or Plan Everything can preserve one temporary stat. Break Their Guard converts the first Breach trigger each turn into energy and draw.
- Scaling speed: high after setup, but spending 3 energy on two Powers against Byrdonis's opening 17 damage cost 11 HP.
- Defensive consistency: Danger Room raised basic Defends to 8-9 Block and We've Trained for This reached 13, but low starting HP left no margin for a missed block draw.
- Current balance flags: strong coupled scaling ceiling; substantial setup burden; Optic Breach remains highly reliable because zero-Block targets trigger both relic and Power.

## Run Assessment

### Character Thesis

Cyclops is a setup-tempo character whose strongest engine couples Breach, temporary stats, and Train. Optic Breach reliably activates Battle Visor and Break Their Guard even against zero Block, creating Vulnerable, temporary Strength, energy, and draw for follow-up attacks. Danger Room supplies repeatable temporary Strength and Dexterity, while Field Training, Plan Everything, Plan 2, and Optic Focus can retain part of those stats. The ceiling is strong once multiple Powers are installed, but the deck pays meaningful opening energy and draw costs, and its Block remains card-play intensive under debuffs that limit energy or cards per turn.

### Strongest Evidence

- Observation: E03, E17, E21, E47, and E48 repeatedly showed Optic Breach or Plan 2's Breach 5 triggering Battle Visor and Break Their Guard against zero-Block targets, applying Vulnerable and temporary Strength while refunding energy and drawing a card.
- Interpretation: The starter Breach package is Cyclops's most reliable engine and does not depend on enemies actually having Block.
- Observation: E19 showed Danger Room plus Break Their Guard costing 11 HP during elite setup, while later turns produced a 46-damage native-card sequence; E47 showed Danger Room+, Permafrost, Optic Focus, and Field Training scaling both offense and defense in a seven-turn elite win.
- Interpretation: Cyclops has a high coupled scaling ceiling, but setup tempo and defensive support determine whether that ceiling is reachable safely.
- Observation: E51 showed a maximum visible 24 Block from three defensive cards against 42 combined incoming damage under Sloth, Waste Away, and Disintegration; no potion remained and the boss had 219 HP.
- Interpretation: The final deck lacked a compact defensive answer to late boss debuffs despite having adequate ordinary-encounter defense.

### Confounders

- Agent misplays: At E51, Sloth's displayed amount 2 represented two cards already played before the round-10 resume. Rapid Punch was played before Defend and consumed the third-card allowance, costing 9 Block. A Defend-first line projects 15 HP entering round 11 instead of 6, but the visible maximum 24 Block still leaves 18 damage and remains lethal. The floor-20 Gambler's Brew multi-select also missed its intended second selection after indices shifted.
- Game variance: Status-heavy encounters repeatedly produced low-action hands; Thieving Hopper removed Plan Everything; Knowledge Demon applied the especially hostile Sloth, Waste Away, and Disintegration combination. The run entered several major fights at low HP.
- Relic or encounter dependence: Permafrost materially covered Danger Room+'s setup turn; Nutritious Soup's free Eternal Strikes and Unceasing Top changed card economy; Akabeko exposed the Call Phoenix/Vigor bug. Booming Conch affected elite openings but not bosses.
- Tooling limitations: Build identifiers were unavailable. Several combats, including Knowledge Demon rounds 1-9, were resumed mid-fight, so full turn histories, exact draw counts, Stable Serum timing, and all card contributions are unknown. Transient `Play Phase: False` states required repeated polling.
- Suspected bugs: E35 cleanly reproduced a Skill consuming Vigor. E38 showed Breach removing Block contrary to its glossary. E24-E25 included additional unresolved displayed-versus-resolved damage anomalies that were not reproduced cleanly.
