## Run Assessment

### Character Thesis

Cyclops's Phoenix Force Visor/Breach engine (Strikes and Breach-tagged attacks bypass Block, break it cleanly, and chain into Vulnerable/temp-Strength via the relic) proved a reliable, low-variance damage floor across all three Acts, reinforced by a deliberately conservative Power-drafting policy (never more than 4 Powers, each picked for fast/same-combat payback per the tempo-bias heuristic) and a steadily-built Block-density card pool (Rally+/Tactical Retreat/Remember Who We Are+/Protection of the Phoenix) that answered this run's single most recurring failure pattern — Block-starved hands in emergency rounds. This combination was sufficient to clear Act 1, Act 2 (including a near-death 9%-HP dual-boss win), and the entire back half of Act 3 (including a 7%-HP Elite win) through skillful, heuristic-consistent play. However, the final Act 3 Boss (Torch Head Amalgam + Queen) introduced two mechanics no prior playtest had encountered — a persistent Bound-restriction status power (Chains of Binding) that constrained turn planning, and an apparently-unbounded self-inflicted Weak/Frail/Vulnerable duration climb (reaching `amount: 92` by round 10) — and the run's ultimate Win was decided not by the deck's core engine or the tested heuristics, but by a high-severity, almost-certainly-unintended interaction between Don't Call Me 'Slim'+ and those inflated debuff durations (E63b), which one-shot the boss from what appears to have otherwise been an unsurvivable position. The deck and heuristics carried the run to the brink of a legitimate win; the anomaly determined the final coin-flip.

### Strongest Evidence

- Observation: E00 — in-state Keyword Glossary text present at embark, defining Breach without requiring inference from play.
- Interpretation: This is a plausible direct improvement over Playtests #1-#3, where Breach's block-bypass-plus-break-trigger behavior had to be independently reconfirmed each run via trial. If the glossary consistently appears whenever a relevant keyword is in play, it should reduce this run's "mechanic confirmed via repeated trial" evidence burden.
- Observation: E58b — Break Their Guard (drafted at E07, Act 1 Floor 4) was drawn and played again mid-combat in the Act 3 Elite fight (Floor 43), confirming Cyclops Powers are per-combat plays, not permanent one-time installs.
- Interpretation: This corrects a mischaracterization baked into this run's own tempo-bias-heuristic reasoning from E07 onward (see Balance Flags below); it is the single most consequential analytical finding of the run for future playtests' reward-decision framing.
- Observation: E63/E63b — Don't Call Me 'Slim'+, played while carrying Weak/Frail/Vulnerable at `amount: 92` each (accumulated over a 9-round Act 3 Boss fight), granted enough temporary Strength to turn a 9-damage Strike into a displayed "Deal 289 damage" attack, one-shotting the 267-HP-remaining final boss (Queen) and converting an apparently-unsurvivable round 10 into an instant win.
- Interpretation: This is assessed as a high-confidence interaction bug (the card's "per stack removed" Strength grant appears to consume each debuff's raw numeric `amount`/duration field rather than counting 1 per debuff type cleared) that single-handedly determined this run's Win outcome. Because the win was substantially enabled by this anomaly rather than by the setup-Power tempo-bias heuristic or keyword-glossary changes under test, the comparison to Playtest #3 (a Loss at Act 2 Floor 22) should be read cautiously: this run's much deeper progress (clearing all of Acts 1-3) is real and partly attributable to the tested changes and generally sound play, but the final Boss fight's survival specifically hinged on an almost-certainly-unintended card interaction, not on the mechanisms the run was designed to evaluate.

### Confounders

- Agent misplays: none causing damage loss this run; one minor card-index-shift near-miss (E58, Precision Shot+ -> unintended Don't Call Me 'Slim'+ play) with no real consequence.
- Game variance: the round-10 Act 3 Boss survival was substantially determined by the Don't Call Me 'Slim'+ anomaly (E63b) rather than by skillful play or the tested tempo-bias/keyword-glossary changes; absent that interaction, available evidence suggests this specific fight was very likely lost at round 10.
- Relic or encounter dependence: the Act 3 Boss (Torch Head Amalgam + Queen) introduced a new Minion/Leader dual-boss archetype and a new persistent player-status power (Chains of Binding / Bound restriction) not seen in any prior playtest, making this encounter's difficulty not directly comparable to Playtest #3's Act 2 Hunter Killer fight.
- Tooling limitations: `get_game_state(format="json")` during combat consistently exceeded the 20KB inline limit (30-49KB observed), requiring a `view`-with-`view_range` step after nearly every single action; this roughly doubled the tool-call cost of combat turns throughout the run. The post-Boss rewards screen also returned an empty `items: []` list (E64), an unexplained deviation from every prior Boss/Elite reward this run. `get_playtest_metrics()` at run end reported `total_records: 0` for the Cyclops mod character (ledger path resolved to a profile-specific file with no entries), suggesting this run's completion was not yet reflected in the telemetry ledger at query time, or the ledger has not been populated for this profile/character combination — flagged as a telemetry limitation rather than assumed to reflect an actual 0-sample-size cohort.
- Suspected bugs: Don't Call Me 'Slim'+'s high-`amount`-debuff interaction (E63b) — see Balance Flags above; this is the run's single highest-severity finding.

### Next Experiments

1. 2. Track whether the Keyword Glossary appears for every keyword this run touches (Vulnerable, Weak, Slow, Tender-equivalent debuffs, etc.), and whether its presence changes the number of combats needed to confirm a mechanic compared to Playtests #1-#3. **Result this run**: the glossary reliably surfaced newly-encountered keywords (Breach at E00, Nimble/Sharp/Swift at E08, and others), but did **not** surface "Bound" with a self-evidently complete definition of its *source* (Chains of Binding was discovered only via manually reading the full player status list mid-fight, at E63) — suggesting the glossary is a solid floor for keyword *effect* text but does not yet extend to explaining a keyword's *origin/trigger condition* when that lives in a separate status power.

Investigate the root cause of Weak/Frail/Vulnerable reaching `amount: 92` in the Act 3 Boss fight — determine whether Queen's Malicious/CardDebuff intent (or some other mechanic) is the source, and whether such large debuff durations accumulating on the player is itself intended behavior or a separate anomaly worth its own bug report.
