### Act 3 Boss: Queen

- Floor and HP: Entered at 67/76 after Blood Vial; died on round 6.
- Deck and upgrades: 37 cards. Accuracy, Phantom Blades+, three Cloak and Daggers, Knife Trap, Snakebite+, Poisoned Stab+, Bullet Time, Malaise, Expertise+, Mirage+, and Anticipate+ were the principal engine cards.

- Defensive consistency: Insufficient under permanent Frail, Weak, Vulnerable, and Bound draw restrictions.
- Current balance flags: Queen's minion pressure plus Chains of Binding can produce terminal draw constraints even after a strong opener.

## Run Assessment

### Character Thesis

This Silent build had high opening throughput and several overlapping engines, but it traded consistency for breadth. Very Hot Cocoa, Ring of the Snake, Bag of Preparation, Bullet Time, Accuracy, Phantom Blades, Poison, and draw effects produced strong turns. The 37-card final deck, reactive defense, and Queen's permanent debuffs made later defensive access unreliable. The run cleared two acts and two Act 3 multi-enemy encounters, but entered the final boss without potions after losing 70 HP to the floor 43 elite.

### Strongest Evidence

- Observation: On Queen round 5, 6 card Block plus Ripple Basin's 4 Block exactly reduced a 24-damage attack to 14, leaving Silent at 1 HP (E16, E20).
- Interpretation: Ripple Basin materially extended the fight, but survival required forgoing a 19-damage retained Shiv.
- Observation: On round 6, the maximum live line generated 16 Block and reduced Torch Head from 125 to 84, against `16x3` incoming damage (E17).
- Interpretation: The terminal turn was not an index or targeting error; the available hand lacked both lethal damage and adequate block.

### Silent/Cyclops Comparison

Both sampled Ascension 3 runs lost on Act 3 floor 48, but their final bosses exposed different failure modes.

| Character | Final deck | Boss turns | Quantitative terminal result                                                                                  | Primary constraint                                              |
| --------- | ---------: | ---------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Silent    |   37 cards |          6 | Queen at 281/400 and Torch Head at 84/199 before the final enemy turn; 16 Block against `16x3` at 1 HP        | Bound draw restrictions, permanent debuffs, and minion pressure |
| Cyclops   |   32 cards |         13 | Cleared Test Subject's 100-HP and 200-HP lives and left its 300-HP life at 20 HP; 34 Block against 42 at 1 HP | Alternating Intangible and status-polluted draw                 |

Cyclops dealt at least 580 of Test Subject's 600 sequential HP, while Silent removed 119 of Queen's 400 HP and 115 of Torch Head's 199 HP before the final turn. This is not a direct damage-rate comparison because the encounters have different mechanics, but it shows that Cyclops reached a much narrower terminal margin. Silent's larger deck was also five cards above Cyclops's 32-card deck.

### Confounders

- Agent misplays: Spiny Toad retaliation was not inspected early enough and caused a 51-HP loss. Reward discipline was loose, contributing to a 37-card final deck.
- Game variance: Queen round 6 offered no legal line to block 48 or deal 125 lethal; the Bound grouping restricted one base Defend.
- Relic or encounter dependence: Very Hot Cocoa, Girya, Accuracy, Phantom Blades, and Bronze Scales materially affected combat arithmetic. Queen and Test Subject are not mechanically equivalent comparison targets.
- Tooling limitations: The public MCP wrapper disconnected, so the same production mod API was exercised directly at `/api/v1/singleplayer`, with polling after every animation-sensitive action. Exact whole-run play counts are unavailable.
- Suspected bugs: Dagger Spray's 14 damage to Torch Head versus 2 to Queen had no surfaced status explanation (E19).

### Next Experiments

1. Repeat Silent A3 with a target final deck size of 25 cards or fewer and preserve at least one defensive potion for the Act 3 boss.
2. Inspect Queen and Torch Head immediately before every all-enemy attack to isolate the E19 damage-routing interaction.
3. Track legal Block per Queen turn before and after choosing the single Bound card.
4. Prioritize one scaling package rather than drafting both broad Shiv and Poison support.
5. Re-test the floor 43 Knights with a potion reserve and compare HP loss against this run's 70-HP baseline.
